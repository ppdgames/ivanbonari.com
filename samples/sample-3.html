First Chapter from Up to Nothing

Dark Monologue, Noir Thriller/Detective

Raindrops are flocking into rivulets and quickly flow down the huge floor-to-ceiling glass. The downpour does not want to come to an end! It has been going on for a whole week, and only occasionally it’s giving people tiny moments of rest. Did our sins overwhelm the patience of that unknown old man, and he still decided to send the second great flood to the Earth? Tempting, too tempting to be true. It's a pity, but I didn't have time to wait for the result at all. I had to rely only on my own strength, and now there are only two bullets left in the clip. Two bullets and two people, you and me. At the moments from cheap crime films, they say that only one should survive, but we are in the reality, in a cruel and dark fucking reality. She will devour us without batting an eye, spit out the remains and not even choke. Yes, I see that you are moving your dry lips, trying to lick them, but there is not enough moisture as there is a nasty stinking lump has accumulated in your throat. Well, never mind, go on as hard as you can, babble and beg for forgiveness, try to buy me off with your dirty money. Isn't that what you're doing right now? So strict, in his lousy business suit and expensive glasses on the tip of his nose. You probably think I'm listening to you, hoping to grab at least some time! It will pass, people tend to make mistakes. It's just a pity that not everyone has time to realize this during the lifetime.

It’s getting to storm. The lightings are still flashing on the other side, in a deprived neighborhood; oh, I wish it to disperse harder and drown out the nasty howl of police sirens. I have almost no strength left, and I have a terrible headache. It seems that there are too many people in uniform gathered in front of the building. They must have blocked all the lanes, stopped a huge river of cars, and now they are putting up barriers and winding their red ribbons between them, warning passers-by about the danger. That's right, I hear a lot of horns, their signals merge into a single dissatisfied hum. People were detained, people were made to wait and they don't want to put up with it. What do they care about what's going on inside a huge skyscraper, because in some half an hour the final game of the season will begin. The bets are made, the beer is hanging out in the trunk, and there's a traffic jam and cops who don't let you get home.

They are already running up the stairs at full speed, in a hurry to quickly drag me into hell. Nothing bad, it's good for them, let them shake their fat collected over the years of service at least a little. It's good that the nearest building opposite stands a little to the side, and you don't have to be afraid of snipers. I'm sorry, Detective Sterlin, but I'm not going to give up anymore. You're probably a great guy, but I'm afraid you're not strong enough to stop this car, whose name is Vickers and Co. The greatest businessman and the most respected criminal authority of the whole City, and maybe the state. I'm sure one word about him was enough to scare off any bandit who knew at least a little about the situation, and the second to make vivid dreamy lights light up in his eyes. Many people wanted to be in this chair, in front of which I stand, an unemployed and unknown resident of our glorious City. Many people, not only you, Jack!? Am I right? You are ready to give your soul, just to get as far away from here as possible!

"Well? Have we agreed?" a slightly older, chalk-pale man with slightly doddering hands stared at me, hiding in the dark, but I didn't even move. "Take everything! Money, company, influence..." the owner of the office wanted to continue, but at that moment a broken line of lightning cut through the air behind him and our views finally crossed.

Now you have neither money nor company, Jack. All that's left are your habits and your brain, unable to think, which tells you the same words over and over again. Round and round, attempt after attempt, anything to prolong the moments of your worthless life. Just to have time to take a few more breaths, and then we'll get out! Really!? Mr. Whiskers, I'm going to upset you, but it's not going to work today. The day of reckoning has come, no matter how pathetic it sounds. It's good that no one hears me...

A trusty pistol, my first weapon. Once it all started with it, and now it will end. The good old M1 Bracket, produced for the needs of the army. Simple, reliable and heavy enough to hit an enemy on the head. Thank you, Mathias, for selling me this magic wand. I hope the coffin is comfortable enough and the worms don't bother you too much. Today, the old man is surprisingly obedient, and the trigger is pliable and soft. I didn't even notice how my index finger did its job. The bullet pierced through Jack's head and broke the glass behind his back. My enemy's chin was knocked back, and his thick and dark blood, mixed with brains and bone, poured onto the floor. That's definitely it now... The old man went to meet his nephew.

There is only one monster left to finish, who looks at me from all the mirrors. Only now it finally dawned on me how hard it is to live next to this person, to see him in every window, to listen to vile speeches and proposals. Wherever I went, wherever I tried to hide, he always followed me, called out and brought me back to reality. How many times did it seem to me that it was time to stop, stop this slaughter, stop committing crimes and calmly leave? I can't remember anymore. But now, now is a completely different matter. I saved this last bullet for him, but for some reason my hands don't obey... The hot barrel left a round burn on his temple, and this bastard is still alive.
